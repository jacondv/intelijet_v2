# 2. Safety Warnings

Read this chapter before operating the machine for the first time. It covers the hazards on this system and what to do if something goes wrong.

**Warning symbols used in this manual:** ⚠️ general hazard · ⚡ electrical hazard · 🔦 laser/optical hazard · ⚙️ mechanical/moving-parts hazard

## The most important thing to know

There are two different controls on this machine — know which one to use:

- **The vehicle's "emergency" control** stops the engine and cuts hydraulic power. **Electrical power stays on** — it does **not** fully power down the machine.
- **Turning off the Ignition, or disconnecting the machine's mass (battery master switch),** is what fully cuts power. This is what makes the Housing safe to move by hand, and it is required before any maintenance.

The on-screen software "Exit" button only stops the software — it does **not** stop any moving part.

**Quick reference:**
| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| Hydraulic arm moving unexpectedly / stuck | Press the vehicle's **"emergency"** control immediately (stops engine + hydraulic supply). This does **not** mean it is safe to touch — see below. |
| Housing pinch | Turn off **Ignition** or disconnect **machine mass** — Housing is then safe to move by hand. |
| Any maintenance or disassembly | Turn off **Ignition** or disconnect **machine mass** first, always. |

## ⚙️ Housing — pinch hazard

**Hazard:** the Housing (the cover over the scanner) opens and closes using an electric motor. It can pinch your hand if you reach into it while it is moving.

**If it happens, do this now:**
1. Turn off the **Ignition**, or disconnect the machine's **mass** (battery master switch) — this is the Housing's power cutoff; there is no separate Housing-only switch.
2. Once power is cut, the Housing can be moved by hand to free your hand or whatever is caught.

There is no fixed safe distance required when starting a scan — just keep your hands out of the Housing's moving zone while it opens/closes.

## ⚙️ Hydraulic arm — crush hazard (NOT the same as the Housing)

**Hazard:** the hydraulic arm (2 joints, 1 cylinder) positions the Housing close to the surface being scanned. It moves independently of the Housing, so it is a separate pinch/crush zone.

**Important — do not treat this like the Housing.** Even after the arm stops moving, it is **not automatically safe to touch**. Unlike the Housing, the arm does **not** release its hydraulic pressure on its own — it can stay pressurized and hold its position.

**If it happens, do this now:**
1. Press the vehicle's **"emergency"** control immediately — this stops the engine and cuts the hydraulic power supply.
2. Do **not** attempt to move or free anything caught in the arm by hand, even after this.
3. Call a technician/authorized person to relieve the hydraulic pressure using the proper release procedure before anyone touches the arm.

> ⚠️ **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** — the exact pressure-release procedure (steps, location of the release valve/control). Do not guess or write these steps until confirmed.

## 🔦 Scanner (laser) — eye hazard

The scanner is a SICK LMS511 laser scanner, **Laser Class 1** — safe for the eye under normal use; no special eye protection is required for this scanner.

## ⚡ Electrical hazard

**Hazard:** the Housing, hydraulic arm, and PLC are all electrically powered.

**Before any maintenance or disassembly of any part:** turn off the **Ignition**, or disconnect the machine's **mass** (battery master switch). This is the machine's main power cutoff — the same action used to make the Housing safe (see above). This applies to any electrical or mechanical work, not only to clearing a jam.

## Personal protective equipment (PPE)

No special PPE is required for normal operation of this system.

## Working in confined spaces

This system is used in confined or limited spaces (e.g., tunnels, pipes). Confined spaces carry extra risks beyond the machine itself — air quality, ventilation, and the ability to communicate with others nearby. No additional site-specific requirements beyond general awareness of this have been identified.
